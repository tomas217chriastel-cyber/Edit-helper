Auto-transcribe & styled subtitles

Click a clip on the timeline first, then generate. The subtitles are rendered as a transparent, animated overlay clip and dropped in above your footage - your original clip isn't touched.

Requires Whisper (pip install -U openai-whisper) and ffmpeg on PATH. For the intended look, install the free Poppins font family (Google Fonts) on your system.

Note: this mode renders the captions as a burned-in video overlay with the pop animation - it looks great but the text itself isn't editable afterward (you'd re-run this to change wording). If you want subtitles you can double-click and edit directly on the timeline, tell me and I'll build a plain-text native-caption mode for you as an alternative (no pop animation, since Premiere's native captions don't support keyframed animation).
